# Operational Weather Product Extension

This extension connects the rainfall and climate prototype to a project-leadership and product-design workflow for operational weather services.

The aim is not to represent MeteoSwiss or internal operational products. It is a portfolio demonstration of how user requirements can be translated into meteorological products and IT deliverables.

## What It Demonstrates

1. Elicit user-oriented operational questions.
2. Structure requirements by mission profile.
3. Map each profile to weather hazards and product drivers.
4. Generate a product matrix and a short product brief.
5. Visualize which hazards matter most for each use case.

## Relation To The Job Ad

This extension addresses:

- project leadership and customer-facing product development
- advising users on suitable weather products
- translating complex meteorological questions into useful services
- Python-based IT/product prototyping
- business-analysis style requirements structuring
